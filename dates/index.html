The Method

Add four numbers, apply one century adjustment, divide by seven, and read off the remainder.

  1. Year part. Take the last two digits of the year.
  2. Quarter part. Add a quarter of those two digits, dropping any remainder.
  3. Day part. Add the day of the month.
  4. Month code. Add the key number for the month.
  5. Century adjustment. 1900s: add 0. 2000s: subtract 1.
  6. Remainder. Divide the total by 7. The remainder is the weekday.

Two gotchas

  • “Leap year” means a real leap year. 1900 was not one — divisible by 100 but not 400 — so January and February 1900 use 1 and 4. 2000 was one.
  • The century adjustment here covers only the 1900s and 2000s, which is exactly the range this page rolls from.
